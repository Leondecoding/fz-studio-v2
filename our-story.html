Hello friends 👋, we are FZ Studio, an architectural designer and an artist, currently based in London, UK.

I paint slow watercolours about light, quiet corners of nature, the small moments that help us come back to ourselves.

My work grows out of two lifelong habits: looking closely at the world, and listening closely to myself.

Architecture taught me to love clear structure, calm spaces and careful lines. Painting allows me to soften those lines – to let light, colour and small movements of water speak for themselves.

I’m drawn to scenes where the world feels still, but full of hidden life.Through watercolour I explore this inner light: the moments when we pause, breathe, and begin to feel at home with ourselves again.

FZ Studio is built for people who are thoughtful and a little tired of noise – people who want a gentler rhythm, and a quiet visual space to return to.

Across different platforms, FZ Studio follows one quiet thread:

  • – on this website, the Gallery holds the finished body of work, Art Prints offers selected reproductions, and Studio Materials shares a small selection of materials used in the practice
  • – on YouTube, I share slow “paint with me” sessions and realistic studies from the studio
  • – on Substack, I write reflections and practice journals about what these moments mean to me

Whether you are here to watch, to read, or to bring a piece of this world into your home, I hope my work can offer you a small pocket of light and stillness in your day.
